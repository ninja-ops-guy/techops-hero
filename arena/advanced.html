Night Walker · Private 1v1

Prototype · directional combat review · first to two round wins

Create a room and share its code with the second player.

Move: A/D or arrows · Jump: Space · Aim: W/S or ↑/↓ · Light: E · Heavy: J · Block: Q · Dash: Shift

All fighters share this base kit. Press light on beat to chain jab, cross and launcher; jump after a confirmed launcher to follow. Down aims aerial attacks. Damage and timing are provisional. Focus spending, specials, grabs, throws, slides and gravity cancels are deferred. No campaign saves are accessed. Controls become active after the countdown.
