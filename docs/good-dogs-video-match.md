# Good Dogs — video reference pass

The supplied September 13 films show realistic small white dogs in fitted dark knit and camouflage, heavy gold chains, dark industrial steel, amber practical lights, and close camera framing. The phone recording also exposed Safari selecting control labels during play.

This pass replaces Katrin and Manchez with continuous faces and limbs, fitted garments and blended quadruped skins; replaces K’s segmented jacket and trousers; and adds 33-frame in-place movement clips. It projects newly generated ivory-fur and worn-steel material scans in rest-pose/world coordinates, adds dense skinned strand cards, and uses closer cameras and stronger motivated amber light. Mobile controls retain large touch targets and suppress native selection/callouts.

These are authored meshes, not photogrammetry or production-finished digital doubles. The external reconstruction service rejected the upload; no reconstructed mesh is claimed. Fine likeness, facial acting, physically simulated cloth, and long fur still differ from the films. Desktop software GPU and emulated touch checks do not certify physical iPhone frame rate.

M5 remains after K’s release. The existing host owns combat, collisions, story, saves and timing. Mike Index → route security → explicit Access Node interaction → native M6 handoff is preserved. The tentacled creature and cockpit footage guide the art; they do not introduce a later boss or skip Waldo’s release.

Editable source: 3D Jutsu project `946e9e0e-32a2-4da2-84fd-25fc51f619b1`, revision 10. Apply `rebuild_video_characters.py`, `rebuild_video_k.py`, then `animate_video_crew.py` to the prior scene. `package_video_models.py` exports per-material quality tiers with interpolated skin weights and normals. The manifest records shipped hashes and triangle counts. Generated texture sources were created for this project; no external texture library was used.

Validation evidence is produced by the Good Dogs video reference renderer workflow: four cameras, both model tiers, performance mode, keyboard reversal, emulated touch, quality persistence, and actual WebGL captures. Its artifact is the visual release review, not the generated studio reference.
