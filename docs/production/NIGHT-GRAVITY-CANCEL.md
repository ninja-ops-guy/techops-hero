# Gravity cancel — offline prototype

In the air, down + dash briefly suspends the character for up to 180 ms of
simulation time. Punch or kick in this window uses the corresponding ground
move and resumes falling. The cancel costs dash cooldown, has no invulnerability,
does not grant an extra jump, and is limited to once per airtime. It may cancel
an attack only after a confirmed hit; a whiff or incomplete multi-contact move
cannot be canceled. Landing re-arms it; getting hit interrupts it without
refunding its use. A jump or window expiry also ends the suspension.

This is initial offline tuning, not a competitive balance or online rollback
claim. Animations reuse existing poses. Full directional light/heavy moves,
Iona's weapon/meter kit, multiplayer authority, physical touch and independent
player versus player testing remain open.
