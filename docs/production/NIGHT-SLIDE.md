# Offline slide prototype

In Night Walker, hold down and use dash (Shift on keyboard, or down on the stick
and the optional dash control). On the ground, this starts a 500 ms slide with
no added invulnerability. The slide shares dash cooldown. After 100 ms on the
simulation clock, jump or attack cancels the slide; canceling clamps retained
horizontal speed and does not reset the cooldown. Taking damage interrupts the
slide. Normal dash and dash-to-grab remain separate.

Slide presentation currently reuses an existing sweep pose. K and Felicia have
incomplete provisional combat visuals; custom slide animations are not done.
Values are initial offline tuning. Online player-versus-player movement, remote
synchronization, fairness under latency, physical-device touch, and balance are
not qualified. Iona's nunchucks, Uzi, parry and Momentum meter are separately
planned and are not implemented in this slice.
