YOUR JOURNEY, CONNECTED

Three steps. One story.

  1. Start in TechOps Hero.

    Play the main game in this browser. Already have a save? Choose Continue, open Linked Story, and link the loaded run when offered. Do not start a new game or clear browser data to connect an existing save.

  2. Discover a new perspective.

    Your game milestones reveal See World chapters. Read at your pace and choose your route. Hidden perspectives stay unnamed until the story reaches them.

  3. Return to Night Walker.

    A completed story objective can earn a new section. Open Linked Story inside Night Walker to enter it. An unlocked section still needs to be played; its completion returns here.

Keep your progress safe.

Use the same browser and device for both experiences. On a phone, open both in your normal browser rather than an Instagram in-app window. Your choices save locally when storage is available; clearing website data removes them. Export progress from Save tools to keep a copy of your linked decisions.

The comic is always available without a game save. See World’s current campaigns are choice-driven stories; the 3D gallery is a separate place to explore.
